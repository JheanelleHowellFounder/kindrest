So I'm not going to pretend I'm sending you a wave of new customers. I asked you

Jheanelle

kindrest.co

## Before you send

- Swap in their name and one real detail from this morning — that one line is
  what makes it obviously not a template.
- Say the "I'm early" part. Don't soften it. It's the reason they'll trust the
  rest of it, and it means nothing you said can bite you in three months.
- Keep the ask to counter space. Don't add a second option.

## If they reply yes

Get the business name to me and I'll set up their link, then you'll have
`kindrest.co/join/theirname` for the cards — a page that says
**Kindrest × Their Name**, with everyone who joins through it tagged to them.
That's how you get to say "twelve moms joined from your counter" in a month.
