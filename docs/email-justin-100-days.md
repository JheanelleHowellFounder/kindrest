# Email to Justin

**Subject:** ok so I actually looked at my numbers

---

Hey Justin,

I finally stopped guessing and pulled the real numbers this week. Some of it was
rough so I'm just going to say it.

19 accounts total. Last signup was June 7. I sent my launch email and posted on
LinkedIn this week and got zero signups from it. Also every single one of my
active users has gone quiet — nobody's checked in for two weeks.

So the thing I thought was an acquisition problem is also a retention problem,
which I did not want to find out. If I'd gotten my 100 users without noticing
that, I'd just have 100 people who tried it once.

The good news is those 17 users each did about 18 check-ins before they drifted,
so the thing does work when people use it. And they were all on the old version.
The new home screen went live August 6, so none of them have even seen it. I'm
emailing them this week — cheapest test I'll ever run.

I also had no analytics installed, which is embarrassing. I couldn't tell you if
that LinkedIn post got 4 visitors or 400. Fixed that today.

Plan is basically: get the loop working on people who cost me nothing, then go
sell pilots. Not the other way around. I set a real checkpoint for Sept 9 — if
retention still looks bad I stop chasing users and go fix the product instead.

Two things I'd love you to poke holes in:

**The paywall.** I don't want to charge for the daily stuff — the check-in, the
care kit, the crisis support. That stays free, full stop. What I'd charge for is
the history and the pattern analysis, the "here's what's actually been helping
you" part. Which also means it only works once someone's been around a while,
so I'm not turning it on yet. Am I being precious about this? I keep wondering
if I should just charge.

**B2B timing.** I did the backwards math and it stung. A pilot has to sign by
Nov 6 for people to be onboarded by my deadline, and mid-market takes 6-12
weeks, so PagerDuty needed to be moving basically now. And APS just can't happen
this year — school district procurement is RFPs and board approval and fiscal
year stuff, that's 6+ months minimum. I'm pulling it out of this plan and
keeping it warm for next year. Didn't love writing that down.

So I'm adding a smaller $1,200 pilot for 15 seats. My $5K price only works for
companies whose buying process is too slow for my timeline. I'd rather have a
founder just say yes.

One ask: I think parents' ERGs are my best door — they usually have their own
budget, no procurement, and they're all people who lived this. If you know
anyone running one, that intro would help more than anything else right now.

Last thing I'm unsure about. I'm not building an ROI deck. With 19 users any
number I make up is fiction and I feel like a real benefits person would see
through it in a second. My plan is to just say "EAPs get like 3-5% usage, you
know that, let me show you our usage every week and we'll find out together."
Feels more honest to me but maybe I'm being naive and people need the business
case to take you seriously? Genuinely don't know.

Anyway. Numbers were worse than I thought in one way and better in another. At
least I know now.

Talk soon,
Jheanelle
